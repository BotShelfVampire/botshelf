# Practical ChatGPT guides by language

These pages explain the same practical Custom Instructions setup in the reader's language. They are documentation, not separate Marketplace products.

Each guide keeps stable preferences in Custom Instructions, puts task-specific facts in the conversation, and includes privacy and verification boundaries.

Language selection is based on current global usage and regional reach. OpenAI reported on June 30, 2026 that more than half of active ChatGPT users primarily use a non-English language, with Spanish, Portuguese, and Arabic leading. Hindi and Indonesian extend practical access across India and Southeast Asia.

Sources: [How ChatGPT adoption has expanded](https://openai.com/index/how-chatgpt-adoption-has-expanded/) · [Supported ChatGPT languages](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8357869-how-to-change-your-language-setting-in-chatgpt)
