- 2026-10-04 (owner hint: cTrader HTF via GetIndexByTime(currentOpenTime) - 1): **implemented with a guard; the bare hint was not enough.** The official reference does not define how `TimeSeries.GetIndexByTime` resolves a time inside a bar (https://help.ctrader.com/ctrader-algo/references/Collections/DataSeries/TimeSeries/ only says "Find the index in the different time frame series"); Spotware staff on the forum describe it as the *nearest/closest* bar. Under floor rounding `k - 1` is closed, but under nearest or ceiling rounding `k` can be the HTF bar *after* the one containing the chart bar (it exists in history), so `k - 1` would be the containing bar, i.e. lookahead on historical bars.
